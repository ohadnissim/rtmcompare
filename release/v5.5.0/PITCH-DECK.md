# RTM — Pitch Deck

*Three apps. One local-only loop.*

---

## Slide 1 — The promise

### Mix. Master. Ship.

*One toolkit for the three things you've been stitching together.*

---

## Slide 2 — The problem

### Audio QC still lives in four apps.

*A meter plug-in for the numbers. A second DAW window for the A/B.
A browser tab with the platform target LUFS. The artist on FaceTime
asking "is it bigger?" RTM collapses all of that into one screen.*

---

## Slide 3 — The other problem

### Your ears lie after hour eight.

*Loudness wars trained an entire generation to mistake louder for
better. By the end of a session, even when you know that, your tired
ears don't.*

---

## Slide 4 — The answer

### Numbers, not vibes.

*Drop a file. See the verdict. Ship the record.*

---

## Slide 5 — The differentiator

### Hear it through Spotify before you ship.

*30 seconds of your master through Apple's actual AAC encoder, after
Spotify's actual normalization, with the 4× true-peak limiter modeled
on Apple's. The truth, before you upload.*

---

## Slide 6 — The receipt

### Fingerprint your mastering pass.

*An 8-character hash of the per-band tonal move. Same hash twice =
identical pass. Catches the 'remaster' that wasn't actually
re-mastered.*

---

## Slide 7 — The clean stems

### BS-RoFormer 4-stem.

*Vocals · drums · bass · other. SDR 9.66 on MUSDB18HQ — up from ~7.5
with Demucs. Cleaner stems power per-stem masking, EQ Preview
level-match, and the Match tab.*

---

## Slide 8 — The privacy

### Your unreleased mixes never leave this machine.

*Local-only by architecture. No telemetry. No phone-home. No analytics.
Your masters don't enter someone else's training corpus.*

## Slide 9 — The architecture

### Three apps. By design.

*RTM Send is a 1 MB plugin on your master bus. RTMcompare is the
analyser. RTMprofile turns your back catalogue into a target reference.
They talk to each other through one local handoff folder.*
