# Upgrade 01 — Radical R&D (reinvent lens)

**Scope:** RTM Suite delivery-readiness. The three prior audits (dsp-double-check,
reinvent-12, dsp-research) already enumerated the bug list. This pass does NOT
re-list them. It adds the category-level judgment only the reinvention lens
surfaces — and it changes the recommended sequence.

---

## The 1% insight the bug-lists missed: the pivot is already half-built, on a broken foundation

The two prior audits framed "RTM Verify — the neutral delivery-certification
layer" as a **moonshot/PIVOT** — a future bet. It is not future. It already
exists in the tree: `python/rtm_certify.py` is a working CLI that emits a
**signed JSON compliance certificate** comparing two masters (TP, LUFS,
generation-loss, streaming-ready booleans) — exactly the C2PA-style cert the
reinvent-12 audit proposed building. RTM has been shipping toward the pivot
without naming it.

That reframes the entire board question. The decision is not "ship the meter now
vs. hold for a pivot we haven't started." It is: **a certification product is
already in the binary, and it is currently unsound in two ways that are fatal to
a certification business specifically** (and that the DSP audits did not rule on
because they are not DSP bugs):

### Net-new CRITICAL A — the certificate signature is cryptographically worthless
`rtm_certify.py:199-231` signs every certificate with a **machine-local symmetric
HMAC key** generated by `os.urandom(32)` and stored at `~/.rtm/certify.key`. The
docstring claims certificates are "unforgeable without access to the signing
machine." That is true and exactly backwards as a value proposition:

- A **recipient cannot verify** the cert without the secret — and the secret is
  never shared (by design, line 203-205). So the "signature" proves nothing to
  the label/distributor/DSP it is sent to. It is a self-signed claim with extra
  steps.
- The **issuer can forge any payload** — they hold their own key. A studio can
  emit a PASS cert for a clipping master and it is byte-valid.
- Every install mints its own root of trust. There is no RTM-anchored chain, no
  revocation, no public verifiability.

For a *meter you sell* this would be a nice-to-have. For a *certification layer*
("Stripe for delivery compliance") this is the **whole product**, and it is
absent. HMAC must become an asymmetric signature (Ed25519) chained to an
RTM-held or customer-held key, with a public verify path. This is the difference
between a cert and a sticky-note.

### Net-new CRITICAL B — two contradictory true-peak verdicts ship in the same suite
The dsp-double-check CRITICAL #1 is the mono-downmix AAC TP bug in
`encoded_preview.py:254/256/290`. But note what `rtm_certify.py:90` does for its
*own* true-peak field: it loads `mono=False` and calls
`comparator._true_peak_and_overs` — i.e. the **correct per-channel path**. So the
suite now emits **two different true-peak verdicts by two different methods** to
the same paying customer: the analysis/encoded-preview panel under-reads
hard-panned transients by ~6 dB, while the certificate reads them correctly. On a
hard-panned master these two RTM outputs will **openly disagree**. A trustworthy
meter that contradicts itself is worse than a wrong one — it destroys the single
asset (numeric authority) the product sells. Fixing #1 is not just a bug fix; it
is **parity enforcement** — every TP surface in the suite must route through one
audited per-channel function. Treat "one true-peak engine" as an architectural
invariant, not three independent fixes.

### Net-new MED C — 4 GB of dead, license-exposed ML weights ship in the bundle
The reinvent-12 audit flagged "4.5 GB dead ML weights for a separator that
doesn't exist." Grounding it: `model-cache/uai_root/models/` holds a 527 MB
`bs_roformer_4stem` **and** a 3.76 GB `mel_band_roformer_4stem` checkpoint
(4.0 GB total), yet `python/separator.py` contains no `mel_band` / `get_backend`
wiring — the larger model is **dead weight**, not just unused. Two consequences a
DSP audit won't raise: (1) it bloats every cross-platform installer 4 GB for a
feature absent from the UX; (2) BS/mel-band RoFormer checkpoints carry
**research/non-commercial provenance risk** — under HARD CONSTRAINT (c)
(commercial-distribution-license-safe), shipping these weights in a paid binary
is a latent legal exposure even if the *code* is MIT. Strip both before any paid
build; if separation is ever a feature, license the weights explicitly.

## The 3 highest-leverage non-obvious upgrades (ranked by edge / effort)

1. **Collapse to ONE measurement engine + tagged-measurement type, then re-emit
   certs from it.** The reinvent-12 audit's "scalar-certainty" finding
   (`{value, valid, reason, provenance}`) is correct, but the highest-leverage
   version is to make `rtm_certify.py` the *single sink*: every customer-facing
   number — analysis panel, encoded preview, AND certificate — flows through one
   audited measurement layer that carries validity + provenance. This fixes
   CRITICAL #1, CRITICAL B (parity), the in-band sentinel-as-real-number bug, and
   gives the cert real fields to sign. One refactor closes four findings and
   *builds the pivot's substrate for free*. **Highest leverage in the suite.**

2. **Make the certificate actually verifiable (Ed25519 + public verify CLI/URL).**
   Replace machine-local HMAC. Cheap (a day), and it is the literal product of
   the pivot. Pair with C2PA manifest embedding so the provenance travels in the
   delivered file, not a side-car JSON. This is the moat: a generative masterer
   (LANDR/RoEx) cannot credibly self-certify neutrality — only a non-generative
   grader can. Patent/defensibility note below.

3. **Specificity gate as a shipped, signed artifact — not just a pre-ship check.**
   The dsp-research audit proposes a ≥95% benign-EQ specificity / ≥90% artifact
   sensitivity gate as an internal release bar. Reframe it as a *customer-facing
   trust SLA*: publish the confusion-matrix and version-pin it inside each cert
   ("graded by RTM engine v8.4.0, validated to 96.2% specificity on benign EQ").
   This converts an internal QA number into the **B2B/SLA wedge** RTM's company
   structure can sell — and pre-empts the mel-L1 false-positive credibility hit
   by disclosing measured error bounds instead of implying zero.

## Patentable / defensible insight

- **Already noted by reinvent-12 and confirmed worth a fast filing:** the in-DAW
  *plugin-hosting capture-and-send loop* — RTMsend hosting a third-party EQ,
  ring-buffer capturing its output, and RPC-streaming it back to the analyzer
  (`RpcServer.cpp:467` is a live JSON-RPC 2.0 surface). File fast.
- **Net-new, stronger claim:** a **non-generative, verifiable delivery-compliance
  certificate keyed to the measurement engine version**, where the grading party
  is provably incapable of altering the audio (read-only meter) — the cert
  asserts *both* the measured values *and* the non-modification property. No
  generative competitor can issue this class of cert without admitting they
  touched the audio. That coupling (neutral-grader + signed non-modification) is
  the defensible white space, and it is broader than the capture-loop claim.

## The moonshot worth a spike + cheapest experiment

**Moonshot:** RTM becomes the **EU-AI-Act delivery checkpoint** — bundle the
verifiable cert with sister-product UAI (AI-music detection) so a single signed
artifact attests *both* "delivery-compliant" *and* "AI-provenance-declared"
ahead of the Aug-2026 regulatory gate. RTM owns the trusted-neutral-grader slot
for the entire audio-delivery pipeline, B2B, recurring.

**Cheapest experiment (no new DSP):** the substrate already exists — `python/uai/`
ships in this very tree, and `rtm_certify.py` already emits a signed JSON cert.
Spike = add one `ai_provenance` field to the existing certificate (call into the
already-vendored UAI surface), swap HMAC→Ed25519, and take the dual-attestation
cert to **three** label/distributor contacts to test willingness-to-pay for a
combined compliance artifact. Days, not weeks, and it validates the entire pivot
thesis before committing engineering to it.

## Verdict contribution (this division)

**CONDITIONAL NO-SHIP as a paid meter until CRITICAL #1 is fixed as a *parity*
fix (one TP engine), not a point patch** — because the suite currently ships two
contradictory true-peak verdicts, and self-contradiction is fatal to a
trust-product specifically. **The certification pivot is not a future bet — it is
half-built and currently unsound** (worthless HMAC signature). Recommended GTM:
do NOT hold the meter for a from-scratch pivot, and do NOT ship the cert feature
as-is. Ship the meter once #1-#4 + parity land; **gate `rtm_certify.py` behind a
flag / remove it from the paid surface until Ed25519 lands**, because shipping an
unverifiable "signed certificate" is a larger brand risk than not shipping one.

**Single biggest risk if shipped as-is:** RTM emits a byte-valid PASS certificate
(unverifiable HMAC) for a hard-panned clipping master that its own analysis panel
*also* under-reads — a paying label delivers it, a DSP rejects it, and the one
asset RTM sells (being the trustworthy meter) is destroyed publicly and
permanently. The cert makes the existing DSP bug *worse*, because it puts RTM's
name and a signature on the wrong number.

**Highest-priority recommendation:** Collapse all true-peak / LUFS / loudness
surfaces (analysis panel, `encoded_preview.py`, `rtm_certify.py`) onto a single
audited per-channel measurement engine carrying `{value, valid, reason,
provenance}` — fixing CRITICAL #1, the cross-surface parity contradiction, and
the sentinel-as-real-number bug in one refactor, and producing the trustworthy
substrate the certification pivot requires.
