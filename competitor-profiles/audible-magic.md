## Product & Features

### Core capabilities
- Automated Content Recognition (ACR) — fingerprinting for copyright management
- "Broad Spectrum" — detects manipulated/altered music (pitch shifts, time-stretch)
- Rights administration and licensing workflow
- Live stream monitoring
- ID360 service for song identification in files and streams

### AI Detection (indirect only)
- **No standalone AI detection product**
- **Udio partnership (April 2025)**: Udio integrates Audible Magic to fingerprint AI-generated tracks at creation time — tracks enter the ACR database tagged as AI-generated
- **Suno partnership**: Suno uses Audible Magic to block copyrighted recordings from being reproduced
- Music.ai integration: Content ID module powered by Audible Magic
- The net effect: AI-generated tracks from Udio/Suno get fingerprinted at source, making downstream detection easier — but Audible Magic doesn't detect AI, it registers AI tracks

### What they do NOT do
- No AI-generated music detection algorithm
- No per-stem analysis
- No certificate generation
- No AI content classifier (they match against database, they don't classify audio origin)

## Competitive Implications for UAI

**Assessment**: Audible Magic is not a direct competitor to UAI. They solve a different problem: "does this track exist in the database?" vs. UAI's "was this track generated by AI?"

**Indirect threat**: If more AI generators follow Udio/Suno in embedding Audible Magic fingerprints at creation time, the industry could standardize on a "register AI at source" model rather than "detect AI at ingestion" model. This would shift the market away from UAI's detection approach.

**Partnership opportunity**: Audible Magic's customer base (Facebook, Twitch, SoundCloud) are natural UAI prospects. A complementary positioning ("Audible Magic for what it is, UAI for whether it's AI") could generate referrals.
