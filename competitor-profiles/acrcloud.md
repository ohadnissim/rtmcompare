## Competitive Implications for UAI

**Where ACRCloud is strong vs. UAI**: Self-service trial access, widest publicly documented generator list, TikTok enterprise relationship, combined copyright+AI, lowest barrier to entry

**Where UAI is strong vs. ACRCloud**:
- 4-stem BS-RoFormer vs. ACRCloud's 2-stem (vocals/accompaniment)
- Published F1=0.998 vs. ACRCloud's undisclosed accuracy
- Certificate generation for legally defensible audit trail
- Desktop app for non-technical distributors
- Patent-pending status
- Drums/bass/other stems analyzed (UAI goes deeper on hybrid detection)

**Opportunities**: ACRCloud's 2-stem analysis is weaker for partial AI (e.g., AI drums over human vocals). UAI's 4-stem analysis is a genuine technical gap to exploit in sales vs. ACRCloud.

**Threats**: ACRCloud's low-friction trial is a competitive threat to UAI's sales cycle. Their TikTok relationship could expand to become the de facto standard for UGC platform detection, where UAI has less presence.
