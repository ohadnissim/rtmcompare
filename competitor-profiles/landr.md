## Positioning & Messaging

LANDR is primarily a distributor and music tools company. They use AI detection to enforce their own AI music policy, not as a product they sell.

---

## AI Policy & Detection

### Policy (as of Feb 2026)
- **Accepts AI-assisted music**: Yes, with disclosure
- **Cap**: Maximum 12 AI-generated songs per subscriber per calendar month
- **Disclosure**: Mandatory at upload
- **Undisclosed AI**: Detection technology flags undisclosed AI content → account deactivation risk
- **Impersonation**: Blocked outright

### Detection technology
- Uses "state of the art detection technology" per their support documentation
- Does not name which vendor/technology they use
- Likely a third-party API (authio, ACRCloud, or IRCAM Amplify) rather than proprietary tech
- Also operates StreamScan (separate product for detecting artificial streaming activity)

### Fair Trade AI Program
- Requires copyright ownership verification for music used to train AI models
- Compares AI outputs against input dataset for plagiarism detection
- Industry-standard compliance tool, not an external product

## Competitive Implications for UAI

**Assessment**: LANDR is a customer prospect, not a competitor. As a distributor, they need AI detection capabilities at ingestion. Their 12-track/month cap creates a manageable volume for compliance, but they still need accurate detection.

**Sales angle**: LANDR uses an undisclosed third-party detector — UAI could displace that vendor by offering better per-stem analysis and certificate generation that reduces LANDR's liability.

**Not a direct competitor**: LANDR does not sell AI detection externally.
