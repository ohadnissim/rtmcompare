## Strengths & Weaknesses

- Only competitor with proven streaming fraud + AI detection combined

### Weaknesses
- AI detection is a secondary product — risk of under-investment
- Zero technical transparency (no accuracy, no API docs, no generator list)
- No self-service API creates adoption barrier for smaller distributors
- No certificate generation — collecting societies need legal defensibility
- No per-stem analysis (misses partially AI tracks)
- Vancouver HQ may create EU regulatory relationship gaps vs. EU-based competitors

## Competitive Implications for UAI

**Where Beatdapp is strong vs. UAI**: Enterprise DSP relationships (iHeart, Audiomack, Beatport), UMG logo, streaming fraud bundling, data scale

**Where UAI is strong vs. Beatdapp**:
- Per-stem analysis capability
- Published accuracy metrics (F1=0.998) vs. complete opacity
- Certificate generation for legally defensible audit trail
- Self-service API + SDK available now
- EU AI Act compliance positioning (EU-focused market)
- Generator identification (UAI names specific generators covered)

**Opportunities**: Target collecting societies that Beatdapp ignores (MLC is a customer but for fraud, not AI detection). Lead with certificate generation and legal defensibility — Beatdapp has nothing there.

**Threats**: Beatdapp's UMG relationship gives them leverage to embed AI detection in major label workflows. If UMG mandates Beatdapp for all distributors, UAI gets squeezed out.
